US National Park Service, Bill von Allmen

Denali Boundary, US National Park Service

After creating my Massanutten terrain map, I wanted to experiment with a different approach to DEM-based cartography. I was inspired by a video from John Nelson in which he created a similar visualization of Yellowstone National Park. Working on this project taught me how much patience and attention to detail can elevate a map, especially when working with DEM data.

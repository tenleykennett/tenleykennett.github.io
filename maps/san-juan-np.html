Sunrise Terrain Map

ArcGIS Pro, Adobe Illustrator

2026

GEBCO Bathymetry Data

I made this map for an advanced cartography course in college. We were challenged with mapping terrain in a unique way. I chose the area around Uncompahgre Peak near the San Juan National Forest after my uncle told me about his experience hiking there. Inspired by his story, I decided to create the map for him.

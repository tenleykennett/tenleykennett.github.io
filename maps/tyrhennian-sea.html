The Tyrrhenian Sea

ArcGIS Pro, Adobe Illustrator

2026

GEBCO Bathymetry Data

I made this map for an advanced cartography course in college. We were challenged with mapping terrain in a unique way. As I often do, I found myself inspired by a John Nelson map that looked just like card stock paper. I chose to map the Tyrrhenian Sea because it had a cool name.
